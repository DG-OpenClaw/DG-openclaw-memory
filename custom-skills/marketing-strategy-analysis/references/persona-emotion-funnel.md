# Persona, Emotion, and Funnel Guide

## Persona fields
- Basic identity / role
- Work or life context
- Current pain
- Desired outcome
- Explicit needs
- Latent needs
- Information sources
- Evaluation criteria
- Fear before purchase
- Trigger to buy
- Trigger to keep using
- Reason to churn
- Message that resonates
- Message that repels

## Emotional analysis rule
Do not stop at labels like “anxious” or “interested”.
Always connect emotion to:
- situation
- expected loss or gain
- decision barrier
- message / experience needed to move forward

## Standard purchase funnel
- Unaware
- Awareness
- Interest
- Research
- Comparison
- Purchase

For each stage, define:
- customer condition
- emotional state
- main question
- friction
- best message
- best channel
- success KPI

## Standard retention funnel
- Post-purchase
- First use
- Habit formation
- Continued use
- Repeat / upsell
- Referral

For each stage, define:
- expected win
- risk of disappointment
- support needed
- proof of value needed
- emotional driver to continue

## B2B note
Separate:
- end user
- internal champion
- manager / approver
- final decision maker

Include emotional factors like:
- fear of failure
- risk to reputation
- need for defensibility
- trust in implementation support

## B2C note
Include emotional factors like:
- self-identity
- convenience
- guilt reduction
- hope / aspiration
- reward / habit feeling
- trust and social proof
