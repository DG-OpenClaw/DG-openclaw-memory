# Framework Definitions

## 1. PEST
Use to explain how external change affects market demand, buyer behavior, and strategic timing.

Evaluate:
- Political: regulation, compliance, subsidies, platform policy
- Economic: inflation, budgets, consumer confidence, investment appetite
- Social: demographics, values, habits, cultural shifts
- Technological: AI, platform shifts, automation, distribution changes

Use when:
- The category is changing quickly
- Regulation or platform dependency matters
- Timing of entry matters

## 2. Market Trend Analysis
Use to determine whether the market is expanding, stagnating, fragmenting, or consolidating.

Evaluate:
- Market size / growth
- Demand drivers
- User behavior changes
- Category expansion or contraction
- Signals of momentum

## 3. 3C
Use as the core market structure frame.

Evaluate:
- Customer: demand, pain, buying criteria, barriers, repeat drivers
- Competitor: value proposition, target, pricing, channels, strengths
- Company: assets, strengths, execution ability, proof points, differentiation

Goal:
Find where customer need overlaps with competitor weakness and company strength.

## 4. Competitor Mapping
Use to visualize position.

Choose meaningful axes such as:
- high price / low price
- high touch / self-serve
- AI-led / human-led
- enterprise / SMB
- brand-led / performance-led

Goal:
Identify crowded zones, white space, and defendable positioning.

## 5. Substitute Analysis
Use to identify what the customer does instead of buying.

Substitutes can include:
- direct competitors
- in-house workflow
- spreadsheets/manual ops
- status quo / postponement
- adjacent categories

## 6. SWOT
Use to connect internal capability with external opportunity.

Rules:
- Strength/Weakness = internal
- Opportunity/Threat = external

Goal:
Translate SWOT into action priorities, not a static grid.

## 7. STP
Use to decide who to target and how to position.

Evaluate:
- Segmentation
- Targeting
- Positioning

## 8. 4P / 4C
Use to compare seller design versus customer reality.

4P:
- Product
- Price
- Place
- Promotion

4C:
- Customer value
- Cost
- Convenience
- Communication

## 9. USP
Use to define the real reason the offer gets chosen.

A valid USP must be:
- important to the customer
- under-owned by competitors
- credibly deliverable by the target company

## 10. JTBD
Use to uncover why the customer “hires” the product or service.

Evaluate:
- functional job
- emotional job
- social job

## 11. Persona
Use to turn a segment into a decision-making person.

Include:
- context
- pain
- goals
- buying criteria
- emotional resistance
- trigger to buy
- reason to stay
- reason to leave

## 12. Emotional Trigger Analysis
Use to identify the feeling-state that moves the customer through the funnel.

Common emotions:
- anxiety
- urgency
- hope
- relief
- convenience-seeking
- loss aversion
- trust
- pride
- belonging
- attachment

## 13. Purchase Funnel Analysis
Use to design movement from awareness to purchase.

Standard stages:
- unaware
- aware
- interested
- researching
- comparing
- purchasing

## 14. Retention / Repeat Funnel Analysis
Use to design movement from first use to repeat purchase and advocacy.

Standard stages:
- immediate post-purchase
- onboarding / first use
- habit formation
- continued use
- repeat / upsell
- referral

## 15. Customer Journey
Use to connect touchpoints, thoughts, emotions, barriers, content, and actions across the full lifecycle.
