# Customer Journey Template

For each stage, capture:
- Stage
- Customer action
- Touchpoint
- Thought
- Emotion
- Barrier
- Needed information
- Recommended company action
- Content / asset needed
- KPI

Recommended stages:
- Unaware
- Awareness
- Interest
- Research
- Comparison
- Purchase
- First use
- Habit formation
- Continued use
- Repeat / upsell
- Referral
